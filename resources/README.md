## Files

- `screenshots/Week_01/2026/` and corresponding week/year folders: the 186 images displayed in the week pages.
- `sources/2026/` and `sources/2019/`: original downloaded official PDFs.
- [Combined screenshot PDF](Fundamentals_AI_Weekwise_Screenshots.pdf): 187 pages, including a linked week/year index. The PDF contains screenshots; explanations are in the week pages.
- [Coverage manifest](metadata/coverage.json): source links, image hashes, and question-to-image mappings.
- [2026 verification](metadata/2026-verification.json): 120 official key matches across the 12 live assessments.
- [Explanation audit](metadata/explanation-audit.json): independent worked checks and corrected explanations.

## Verification and limits

The audit recomputed the puzzle route and expansion count, alpha-beta bounds, weighted shortest paths, decision-tree entropy and information gain, Bayes calculations, expected utilities, truth tables, and a Q-learning update. Ambiguous or incorrect public answers are identified in their discussions. This does not turn public selections into official grading keys.

The 2024 video sources are linked to course noc24_ge47 and show the corresponding weekly assignments. Their question-column screenshots preserve source annotations and overlay text. Timestamp links beneath each image make its origin reviewable.

No authenticated final-exam paper was located. The repeat map records assignment examples; it does not estimate an exam repeat rate.
