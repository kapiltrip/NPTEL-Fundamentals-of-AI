## Files

- `screenshots/Week_01/` through `Week_12/`: the 113 images displayed in the week pages.
- `sources/`: original downloaded official PDFs for 2026 and 2019.
- `Fundamentals_AI_Weekwise_Screenshots.pdf`: 114 pages, including its linked week/year index. The PDF contains screenshots; the explanations are in the week pages.
- `coverage.json`: source links, image hashes, and per-week counts.
