## Verification

The 2026 official PDF keys matched all 120 accepted answers in the enrolled course on 21 October 2026. The source search and explanation audit were updated on 22 October 2026.

Worked checks cover puzzle moves and expansion counts, alpha-beta bounds, shortest paths, entropy and information gain, Bayes calculations, expected utility, truth tables, and Q-learning. Source errors and ambiguous wording are flagged next to the questions.

Public video selections are not official grading feedback. Their original annotations are preserved, and timestamp links beneath the screenshots identify the frames. No authenticated final-exam paper was found.
