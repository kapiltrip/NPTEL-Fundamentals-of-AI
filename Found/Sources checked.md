### Question-by-question check of the 2023 Week 0 quiz

| Source question | New content | Independent result |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | A card is a three or a heart | $4/13$ |
| Q2 | Museum/cruise union with probabilities $0.36$, $0.47$, $0.22$ | $0.61$; absent from choices |
| Q3 | Choose matrix entries so that $A^2=-I$ | $c=-6$, $d=-1$ |
| Q4 | Angle between two specified vectors in $\mathbb R^4$ | $3\pi/4$ |
| Q5 | Three fair dice all show six | $1/216$ |
| Q6 | Identify a false Hermitian-matrix statement | $A^2=I$ need not imply $A=I$ |
| Q7 | Match the eigenvalues of two specified matrices | $(a,d)=(4+\sqrt6,4-\sqrt6)$ or swapped; absent from choices |
| Q8 | Event union with probabilities $3/8$, $1/2$, $1/4$ | $5/8$ |
| Q9 | Six successive matrix-vector products in five dimensions | Always linearly dependent |
| Q10 | Five of ninety-five raffle tickets win both prizes | $2/893$, assuming distinct winning tickets |

None of these numerical problems or matrix questions occurs in the main collection. The Week 7 single-die question concerns an even outcome, rather than three simultaneous sixes. Q2 and Q8 use the same probability principle but different supplied data; both are distinct source questions, as allowed by the collection's comparison rule.
